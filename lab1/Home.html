A paragraph is a self-contained unit of writing, typically several sentences long, that develops a single main idea,
starting with a topic sentence that introduces the concept,
followed by supporting sentences that explain, describe,

all working together to create a unified and coherent section of a larger text.

like describing your family, explaining a hobby,
